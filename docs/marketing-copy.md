# Marketing copy

This document is the copywriting source of truth for the public marketing site at `/`. It is based on the provided BayaNova Cooperative reference and should be consulted before changing marketing text.

## Voice and positioning

- Present BayaNova as a cooperative and community platform, not only as a welfare-assistance membership product.
- Emphasize shared opportunities, participation, contribution, community programs, and growth.
- Use warm, clear, community-centered language grounded in Bayanihan.
- Prefer “members and communities” over claims that imply guaranteed personal benefits.
- Keep copy inclusive of members, families, schools, organizations, businesses, community groups, and community leaders.

## Core messages

- “Be Part of a Community That Grows Together.”
- “More Than a Cooperative. A Community Moving Forward.”
- “Because for us, being a cooperative means looking beyond ourselves.”
- “Connect. Participate. Contribute. Grow.”
- “Together, we can create opportunities. Together, we can build communities. Together, we can grow.”
- Membership should be presented as participation in the BayaNova Cooperative, not primarily as a list of financial assistance amounts.

## Program language

Use these program themes when describing community work:

- Community Outreach
- Livelihood & Skills Development
- Community Development
- Education & Awareness
- Feeding & Community Support

## Copy-only change rule

Marketing copy may be revised without changing layout, styling, assets, route IDs, or interaction behavior. If a copy change requires a new section, new interaction, new asset, or a new claim about a service, document and review that change separately.
