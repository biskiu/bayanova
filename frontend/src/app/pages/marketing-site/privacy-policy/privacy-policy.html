Our commitment to you

BayaNova respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us by our members, beneficiaries, website visitors, and community partners. We process personal data in accordance with applicable Philippine data privacy laws.

01

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you inquire, register, maintain a membership, or submit a request for assistance. This may include:

  • Identity and contact details, such as your name, birthday, address, email, and phone number.
  • Membership and beneficiary details, including your relationship to covered individuals.
  • Payment and transaction records related to registration or contributions.
  • Documents needed to verify eligibility or process a qualified assistance request.
  • Technical information such as device type, browser, IP address, and website activity.
info

We only ask for information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services and meet our legal obligations.

02

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, verify, and manage your BayaNova membership.
  • Process contributions, requests for assistance, and member support inquiries.
  • Send service notices, account updates, and important program communications.
  • Protect our members, prevent fraud, and maintain the security of our services.
  • Improve our website, programs, and community experience.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful government requests.
03

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share only what is necessary with trusted service providers that help us operate our platform, process payments, communicate with members, verify documents, or deliver assistance. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect a person’s safety, or to defend BayaNova’s legal rights.

Service providers are expected to use personal information only for the agreed purpose and protect it appropriately.

04

How we protect your data

We use reasonable organizational, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from loss, unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we regularly review our practices and limit access to people who need the information to perform their work.

06

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, resolving concerns, maintaining necessary records, and meeting legal or regulatory requirements. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

07

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will provide an appropriate notice.
