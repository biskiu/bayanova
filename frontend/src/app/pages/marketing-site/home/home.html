menu_book OUR STORY

How BayaNova began

BayaNova was founded by community organizers who saw how quickly a medical emergency or the loss of a loved one could push an ordinary Filipino family into debt. Traditional insurance felt distant and full of fine print — the opposite of how Filipinos have always supported one another.

Drawing from Bayanihan, where neighbors carry each other's burdens together, the founders built a simple system: members contribute a small, regular amount into a shared fund used to help members during qualified emergencies.
