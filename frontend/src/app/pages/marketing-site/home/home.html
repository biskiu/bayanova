play_circle OUR STORY

How BayaNova Began

BayaNova began with a simple question:

“What can we accomplish when we choose to grow together?”

We grew up knowing the Filipino spirit of Bayanihan—the neighbor who brings food when a family is struggling, the community that comes together after a loss, and ordinary people who give whatever they can because they know that someday, someone may need their help too.

That spirit is at the heart of BayaNova.

We are building a community where care is shared, hope is passed forward, and no one has to carry life’s burdens alone.

From supporting families during difficult times to giving back through feeding programs, relief efforts, education, and other community initiatives, BayaNova exists to remind us of something simple:

We grow stronger when we work together.
